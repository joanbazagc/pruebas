    Create a Labor Agreement in Omne RH Payroll¶

    Create a labor agreement to define the collective compensation conditions that will be applied to employees assigned to it.

    Prerequisites¶

    • Salary concepts are defined in the salary concepts master.
    • Professional categories are created with a contribution group assigned.
    • You know the agreement publication data (official gazette number, validity dates).

    Steps¶

    1. Choose the search icon, enter "Labor Agreements", and choose the related link.
    2. Select New to create a new agreement card.
    3. In the Code field, enter a unique identifier (for example, METAL-BCN).
    4. In the Application Date field, enter the start date of the first salary table.
    5. Fill in the Description, Social Security Code, Weekly Hours, and Annual Hours fields.
    6. In the Validity section, fill in the Official Gazette No., Publication Date, In Force From, and In Force Until fields.
    7. Mark the territorial scope: State, Provincial, or Autonomous Community as applicable.
    8. In the Terms section, fill in Vacation Days and Vacation Day Type (Natural or Working days).
    9. In the Configuration section, select the Extra Pay Calculation method and configure the guaranteed salary flags as needed.
    10. In the lines subpage, create one line for each category and salary concept combination:
      • Select Category (or leave blank to apply by salary level).
      • Select Salary Concept. The system automatically inherits 13 fields from the concept master.
      • Enter the Amount or the Calculation Formula.
    11. Repeat for all categories and concepts in the agreement.

    Result¶

    The agreement card shows all sections filled in, and the salary table lines appear in the lower subpage. The agreement is ready to be assigned to employees.

    Important notes¶

    • Amount vs. Coefficient: These are mutually exclusive. If you enter a coefficient, the amount must be zero. Additionally, coefficients require a category to be specified on the line.
    • Calculation formula: Uses tokens representing salary concept codes (resolved to the concept amount in the scheme) and #1 for the annual working hours from Payroll Config.
    • Application date required: You cannot insert a line without first setting the application date in the header.
