    Troubleshooting Labor Agreements in Omne RH Payroll¶

    Solutions for common issues when working with labor agreements.

    Amounts do not update after applying the agreement¶

    Symptom: You run the Apply action, but some perception scheme lines keep their old amounts.

    Cause: The Agreement Change flag is disabled on those lines. This means they are protected as voluntary improvements and the system correctly skipped them.

    Solution: - If the protection is intentional, this is expected behavior. - If the protection is accidental, enable the Agreement Change flag on the affected lines and run Apply again.

    Error when assigning a vacation pay¶

    Symptom: When filling in the Vacation Pay No. field in the agreement header, the system shows an error.

    Cause: The selected pay is not of type Vacation. The field only accepts pays of type Vacation.

    Solution: Select a pay that is of type Vacation, or create a new pay with that type.

    Error message: "Pay No. %1 is not on vacation"

    Error when entering a coefficient¶

    Symptom: When filling in the Coefficient field on an agreement line, the system shows an error.

    Cause A: The line does not have a category assigned. Coefficients are only allowed on lines with a category.

    Cause B: The line has a non-zero amount. Coefficient and amount are mutually exclusive.

    Solution: - For Cause A: Assign a category to the line before entering the coefficient. - For Cause B: Set the amount to zero before entering the coefficient.

    Error messages: - "You should not enter coefficients in the salary levels" - "To enter coefficient, leave the amount at 0"

    Salary table lines disappear¶

    Symptom: The agreement card lines subpage appears empty even though the agreement has salary data.

    Cause: The application date in the header does not match any date that has lines defined.

    Solution: Choose the Last Agreement Date action to reposition to the most recent salary table, or use Agreement Dates to select the correct date.

    Formula does not generate an amount¶

    Symptom: A calculation formula is defined on a line, but the resulting amount is zero.

    Cause: - The salary concepts referenced in the formula tokens do not exist in the employee scheme or have zero amounts. - Annual working hours are zero in Payroll Config (if the formula uses token #1). - Circular dependency between formulas.

    Solution: 1. Ensure the base concepts referenced in the formula have amounts in the perception scheme. 2. Fill in the annual working hours in Payroll Config. 3. If there is a circular dependency, restructure the formula to eliminate the cycle.

    Part-time amounts are incorrect¶

    Symptom: After applying the agreement, amounts in the perception scheme are proportionally lower or higher than expected.

    Cause: The Weekly Hours value in the agreement header or in the employee contract is incorrect or zero.

    Solution: Correct the weekly hours in the agreement header and/or in the employee contract. Run Apply again.

    Professional category does not appear in lookups¶

    Symptom: When searching for a category in a lookup field, the category does not appear.

    Cause: The category has the Blocked field set to Yes, which excludes it from lookups.

    Solution: If the category should be available, clear the Blocked field. If it should remain hidden, this is expected behavior.

    Salary level lines not applied to employee¶

    Symptom: Lines defined by salary level (without category) are not reflected in the employee scheme, even though the employee's category has that salary level.

    Cause: A line exists for the same category and same salary concept, which takes priority over the salary level line. The system applies category-level lines first.

    Solution: If you want the salary level line to apply, remove the category-level line for that concept. If both should coexist, adjust the amounts on the category-level line.
