    Bizneo Integration Overview in Omne RH Payroll¶

    Bizneo integration connects Omne RH Payroll with Bizneo HCM and Bizneo ATS. It supports the daily flow of employees, documents, contracts, and the return path from Bizneo into Omne RH through the integrated employee staging tables.

    What this integration covers¶

    • Create or update employees in Bizneo from Omne RH
    • Import employees from Bizneo back into Omne RH as payroll employees
    • Send payroll documents and HR documents to Bizneo
    • Synchronize contracts and document metadata
    • Review integration logs and job queue runs

    Main business flow¶

    1. A payroll employee is copied into Employee Integrations.
    2. The integration sends the employee to Bizneo when the configured mode allows it.
    3. Bizneo returns identifiers and status updates for the employee and related contracts.
    4. Imported data is applied back to payroll employees and their auxiliary records when needed.

    Who uses this module¶

    Role Main use
    Payroll administrator Review employee synchronization and send manual updates
    HR manager Manage employee data, documents, and returned employee records
    Functional consultant Configure integration modes and troubleshoot Bizneo behavior

    Key concepts¶

    Payroll employee¶

    The payroll employee is the master employee record in Omne RH. It is the source of truth for most employee data.

    Employee Integrations¶

    This is the staging area used by the Bizneo integration. It stores employees before they are sent to Bizneo and also stores records received from Bizneo before they are imported into payroll.

    Perceptor transfer¶

    The preferred flow is to transfer a payroll employee into Employee Integrations instead of creating an integrated employee manually. That preserves the link with the payroll employee and keeps the synchronization traceable.

    Auxiliary data¶

    When Bizneo data is imported back into Omne RH, the system can also complete auxiliary records such as additional employee information, income tax data, and bank data.
