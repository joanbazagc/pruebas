    Set Up Income Tax Withholding for a Fiscal Year in Omne RH Payroll¶

    At the beginning of each fiscal year, you must configure the income tax parameters with the latest regulatory values published in the official gazette. This ensures that withholding percentage calculations are accurate for the new year.

    Prerequisites¶

    • Company configured with IRPF = Yes in company setup
    • At least one tax administration created (State or Regional)
    • Perception keys configured with fiscal treatment assigned
    • Employees registered with social security units and work centers

    Step 1: Configure IRPF parameters for the fiscal year¶

    1. Choose the search icon, enter "Configuracion IRPF", and choose the related link.
    2. Set the Year field to the current fiscal year.
    3. Configure special percentages:
    4. Administrators and board members %: Fixed withholding percentage for administration roles
    5. Professional income %: Withholding percentage for professional activities
    6. Arrears %: Percentage for previous year arrears
    7. Minimum % contracts < 1 year: Minimum for temporary contracts
    8. Minimum % special relationships: Minimum for special labor relationships
    9. Configure work income reductions (limits and amounts for the four brackets).
    10. Configure personal and family minimums:
    11. General personal minimum, increase for over 65
    12. Disability minimums (33-65% and >65%)
    13. Dependents minimums (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th+ child)
    14. Children under 3, children with disability
    15. Ascendants minimums (>65, >75, with disability)
    16. Configure family situation thresholds (exemption limits by marital status and children).

    Step 2: Load retention tables¶

    The retention tables contain the progressive tax brackets.

    1. Choose the search icon, enter "Tabla retenc. IRPF", and choose the related link.
    2. For each tax bracket, create a line with:
    3. Year: Fiscal year
    4. Regional tax authority: State or the corresponding regional territory
    5. Income amount: Lower limit of the bracket
    6. Retention quota: Fixed quota for the bracket
    7. % Remainder: Marginal percentage for the excess
    8. Children 0 to 11+: Percentage by number of children (for alternative calculation)

    If your company operates in a regional tax territory, load brackets for both the state regime and each applicable regional authority.

    Step 3: Verify Form 145 data for employees¶

    1. Choose the search icon, enter "Inform. IRPF Perceptores", and choose the related link.
    2. Verify that each active employee has at least one communication recorded with:
    3. Family situation: filled in
    4. Number of children: correct count
    5. Spouse NIF: if status is "With dependent spouse"
    6. Disability degree: if applicable
    7. Mortgage: if applicable

    Step 4: Verify the tax administration¶

    1. Choose the search icon, enter "Administraciones Hacienda", and choose the related link.
    2. Verify each administration has:
    3. Monthly settlement folder: Path for model 111 files
    4. Quarterly settlement folder: Path for model 111T files
    5. Tax domicile: Required for electronic file generation

    Result¶

    The income tax module is ready for the new fiscal year. You can now calculate withholding percentage proposals for employees.
