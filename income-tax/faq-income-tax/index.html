    Frequently Asked Questions About Income Tax in Omne RH Payroll¶

    What is the difference between "Change all" and "Change marked" when applying proposals?¶

    • Change all applies the proposed percentage to all lines for the year. Use it at the start of a fiscal year when the entire proposal is reviewed and approved.
    • Change marked applies only to manually marked lines. Use it for selective changes: only employees with significant variation, or after individual corrections.

    In both cases, if an employee has a fixed percentage different from the proposal, the system asks for confirmation before modifying it.

    What happens if an employee has a fixed percentage and the proposal calculates a different one?¶

    When the employee has the "Fixed % current" field active, it means they requested a fixed percentage via Form 145 (above the legal minimum). The system handles this differently:

    • If the fixed % is higher than proposed: the system asks whether you want to change it.
    • If the fixed % is lower than proposed: the system also asks, because the fixed rate might be outdated.

    The fixed percentage is a voluntary request from the employee and cannot be modified automatically without verification.

    Why does the system calculate different percentages for two employees with the same salary?¶

    The withholding percentage depends on multiple factors beyond salary:

    • Family situation (with dependent spouse, without spouse, single with children)
    • Number and age of children (under 3 have additional reductions)
    • Disability of the employee or dependents
    • Tax administration (state vs. regional with different regulations)
    • Contract type (contracts under 1 year have a different minimum)
    • Special circumstances (geographic mobility, Ceuta/Melilla, housing mortgage)

    All this data comes from the Form 145 registered for each employee.

    When should I load retention tables for a new year?¶

    Load tables and configuration parameters at the start of each fiscal year, when new regulatory values are published in the official gazette (BOE). You do not need to delete the previous year's configuration; create a new one for the incoming year.

    For regional tax authorities, values are published in their respective official bulletins and may have different dates and amounts.

    What is the difference between the IRPF journal and the percentage change journal?¶

    Journal Purpose Result
    IRPF Journal Record withholding movements external to payroll Payroll headers of type IRPF
    Percentage change journal Calculate and apply new withholding percentages Update of the IRPF % in the employee card

    They are complementary tools: one records amounts, the other manages percentages.

    How does the calculation work for regional tax authority employees?¶

    The calculation engine automatically detects the regional authority through the tax administration assigned to the employee's work center. Each territory (Bizkaia, Gipuzkoa, Alava, Navarra) has its own retention tables, reductions, and minimums. You must load IRPF configuration and retention tables for each regional authority in use.

    What is a "Calculation Type" and when is each value used?¶

    Calculation Type When to use
    Initial First proposal of the year, without previous payroll data for the fiscal year
    Regularization Recalculation due to mid-year circumstance changes
    With Payrolls Recalculation considering payrolls already calculated in the year
    Without Pending Remuneration Calculation without considering pending remunerations
    Annual Final calculation at year-end close

    The calculation type is selected in the percentage change journal and affects how the engine estimates the employee's income.
