    Troubleshooting Payroll Calculation in Omne RH Payroll¶

    Solutions for common problems encountered during payroll calculation.

    Recipient without active contract¶

    Symptom: The employee is skipped and the error log shows "Recipient without active contract".

    Cause: The engine cannot find a contract with dates covering the calculation period.

    Solution: 1. Choose the search icon, enter "Employees (Payroll)", and choose the related link. 2. Open the employee card and navigate to Contracts. 3. Verify a contract exists with: - Start Date on or before the period start - End Date on or after the period end (or blank for permanent) - Company Leave blank or on or after the period start 4. Create or renew the contract if needed, then recalculate.

    No scheme for the period¶

    Symptom: The error log shows "There is no scheme for period".

    Cause: The engine cannot find a perception scheme record with an application date on or before the period start.

    Solution: 1. Verify the employee has a perception scheme assigned. 2. Check that the Application Date is on or before the first day of the period. 3. Verify the Labor Agreement in the scheme matches the active contract. 4. If the agreement changed recently, create a new scheme key with the correct application date.

    Days contributed are zero or incorrect¶

    Symptom: The payroll header shows "Days Contributed = 0" or an unexpected number.

    Cause: Calendar not configured (part-time), "Skip day 31" setting active, or incidents absorbing all days.

    Solution: - For part-time: verify the employee calendar has correct work days defined. - For day 31 issue: choose the search icon, enter "Company Configuration", and check the "Skip day 31 monthly payment" field. - For incidents: verify incident dates are correct; incorrect dates may absorb the entire period.

    Social security base does not match manual calculation¶

    Symptom: The common contingencies base differs from the expected manual calculation.

    Cause: The base includes prorated extra pay, IT bases from the previous month, and is subject to maximum/minimum limits.

    Solution: 1. In payroll lines, filter by concepts with "Subject to SS = Yes" and sum the partial amounts (this is the contributable remuneration). 2. Add the proration (visible in the header Proration field). 3. If there is sick leave, add the previous month IT base. 4. Compare with contribution group limits. The engine applies minimums and maximums automatically. 5. For part-time, limits are calculated per contracted hour.

    Income tax withholding is zero¶

    Symptom: The payroll calculates correctly but income tax retention is 0.

    Cause: Withholding percentage is 0%, all concepts are tax-exempt, or perception keys are missing.

    Solution: 1. Check the Withholding Percentage in the employee card (Income Tax tab). 2. If 0%, calculate or manually assign the income tax percentage. 3. Verify scheme lines have a Perception Key assigned (usually "A" for employment income). 4. Verify salary concepts are not all marked as "Tax Exempt".

    Advances are not deducted¶

    Symptom: The employee has pending advances but the payroll does not include a deduction line.

    Solution: 1. Verify advances were registered with Operation Type = Advances (value 4). 2. Check that the advance lines have the "Pay Number" field empty (indicating not yet deducted). 3. Verify the advance period is within the current calculation period range.

    Sick leave benefits are incorrect¶

    Symptom: Social security benefit and company supplement amounts do not match expected values.

    Cause: The previous month daily base is incorrect.

    Solution: 1. Choose the search icon, enter "Incidents", and choose the related link. 2. Verify the Common Contingencies field contains the correct previous month base. 3. Verify Days Contributed is correct (usually 30). 4. If bases are wrong, modify the incident start date and re-validate to recalculate bases. 5. Verify benefit brackets (social security % and company %) are correctly configured. 6. Void and recalculate the payroll.

    Cannot void a posted payroll¶

    Symptom: The system prevents voiding because the payroll is posted.

    Solution: 1. First reverse the corresponding accounting entry from the accounting module. 2. Once reversed, the payroll returns to "not posted" state. 3. Proceed to void the payroll normally. 4. Correct data, recalculate, and re-post.

    Batch calculation is very slow or hangs¶

    Symptom: Batch processing takes excessively long or appears stuck.

    Cause: Large employee volume, record locking from concurrent processes, or orphaned records in the intermediate memory table.

    Solution: 1. Verify no other user is running a simultaneous calculation on the same employees. 2. Split the calculation into smaller batches (by contribution unit or employee range). 3. If the problem persists, contact the system administrator to check database locks.

    Back pay complementary calculates zero difference¶

    Symptom: A back pay complementary payroll shows 0 for all amounts.

    Solution: 1. Verify the labor agreement salary tables were updated with the new amounts. 2. Check that the Application Date of the new table is on or before the back pay period start. 3. Review salary concepts: those marked "Not Retroactive = Yes" are correctly excluded from the difference calculation.

    Extra pay proration is missing¶

    Symptom: The payroll header shows "Proration = 0" and the contribution base does not include extra pay proportion.

    Solution: 1. Verify the perception scheme has lines for extra pay concepts. 2. Check that extra pay lines have amounts assigned. 3. If extra payments are paid separately (type "Extra Pay"), proration in the monthly payroll is 0 (this is correct behavior).
