    Troubleshooting Perception Schemes in Omne RH Payroll¶

    Pending lines in the Change Journal¶

    Error: "You have pending lines in the Change Journal"

    This error appears when you try to calculate payroll from the scheme card but there are unposted lines in the perception journal for this employee.

    Solution¶

    1. Choose the search icon, enter "Perception Journal Sections", and choose the related link. Then choose Edit Journal.
    2. Find the pending lines for the affected employee.
    3. Either post the lines (F9) to incorporate the values, or delete them if they are not needed.
    4. Return to the scheme card and retry the payroll calculation.

    Must inform month to register¶

    Error: "Must inform month to register"

    This error appears when running Collect Variable Perceptions without specifying a month.

    Solution¶

    Enter a value between 1 and 12 in the Month field when running the Collect Variable Perceptions action. If you see "Wrong month", verify the value is within the valid range.

    Variable perceptions remain at zero after posting¶

    Symptom: You collect variable perceptions and post with F9, but amounts in the schemes stay at zero.

    Solution¶

    1. When running Collect Variable Perceptions, disable the Clear Previous Month Quantity and Clear Previous Month Amount options if you want to keep previous values.
    2. Verify that the Period Start and Period End dates on each journal line are consistent with the working month.
    3. Post again with F9.

    New line created instead of updating existing¶

    Symptom: After posting the journal, a new line appears in the scheme for the same concept instead of updating the existing one.

    Solution¶

    The system matches by salary concept + accounting group + period overlap. Verify that:

    1. The salary concept on the journal line matches the one in the scheme.
    2. The accounting group is the same.
    3. The date range (Period Start / Period End) on the journal line overlaps with the existing scheme line.

    Cannot anticipate to net with different periods¶

    Error: "Error, cannot be anticipated to net. There are 2 different periods"

    This occurs when lines marked as Net Advance in the same posting belong to different months.

    Solution¶

    Post advances from each month separately. Alternatively, change conflicting lines to Gross advance type if they do not require social security and tax deductions.

    Missing advance fields¶

    Error: "You must inform the deduction code of the advance" or "You must report the book and section of the payroll journal"

    These appear when lines are marked as Advance but required fields are empty.

    Solution¶

    Before posting, fill in these fields on the advance line:

    1. Advance Deduction Code
    2. Payroll Journal Book
    3. Payroll Journal Section

    Scheme not included in batch payroll¶

    Symptom: The employee is skipped when running batch payroll calculation.

    Solution¶

    1. Choose the search icon, enter "Perception Scheme Headers", and choose the related link.
    2. Find the affected scheme header.
    3. Disable the Sporadic option.
    4. Run the batch calculation again.

    Annual Gross Amount shows zero or incorrect value¶

    Symptom: The scheme header shows zero or outdated annual amounts even though lines have correct values.

    Solution¶

    The Annual Gross Amount is a calculated field (FlowField) that may not refresh automatically in certain navigation scenarios. Close and reopen the scheme card from Perception Scheme Dates to force recalculation.

    Process canceled by the user¶

    Error: "Process canceled by the user"

    This occurs when a duplicate concept is detected during journal posting and neither Replace nor Add was selected.

    Solution¶

    When the duplicate dialog appears, choose one option:

    • Replace: Overwrites the existing value.
    • Add: Accumulates the new amount on top of the existing one.

    If neither option is appropriate, remove the duplicate lines from the journal before posting.
